Hours to Go

Privacy and terms

Last updated 29 October 2026

Hours to Go keeps your logbook so it can show what’s left for each licence and rating. It has no ads and no tracking, and it never sells or shares your information except with the services that run it, listed below.

Who runs it

Hours to Go is made and run by Berk Yilmaz, an independent developer. It isn’t a company, a flight school or Transport Canada. For anything on this page, use Send feedback on the Profile tab of the app.

What’s stored

Without a profile, your logbook stays in your browser and nothing is sent to us. Clearing the browser’s data for this site erases it.

With a profile, these are kept on our server:

  • Your email address.
  • Your password, scrambled one way (scrypt), so nobody can read it, us included.
  • Your logbook: each line’s date, aircraft, registration, airports, times, takeoffs and landings, approaches and remarks.
  • Your settings: the licences and ratings you hold and their dates, your medical category, the milestones you’ve ticked with their dates and exam marks, and what you’re working on.
  • While you’re signed in, a code that keeps you signed in for 90 days after you last opened the app. Only a one-way scramble of it is stored. It is the only cookie Hours to Go sets.
  • When you ask for a password reset link, a one-way scramble of the link, for one hour.

When you send feedback, your message, the email address you give (or your profile’s) and your browser’s name are emailed to the developer. They aren’t kept in the app.

Like every website, the services below log the IP address and the requests that reach them, and keep those logs for a short time to keep things running and secure.

What it’s used for

Only to run Hours to Go: to show your logbook and progress on any device you sign in on, to keep you signed in, to send the emails you ask for, and to answer your messages. No analytics, no advertising, no profiling.

Where it’s kept

These services store or handle your information for Hours to Go. All of them are in the United States, so the information may be accessed under US law.

  • Neon: the database, on Amazon Web Services in Ohio.
  • Render: runs the server, in Ohio.
  • Resend: sends the emails.
  • GitHub: serves this website, and keeps the nightly backups in a private repository.
  • Google: the developer’s email inbox, where feedback arrives.

How long

Until you delete your profile. Deleting it removes your profile and every logbook line from the database at once. Copies in the nightly backups are kept for 90 days, then they’re gone too. Feedback stays in the developer’s inbox unless you ask for it to be deleted.

Your choices

  • A copy of your logbook: Profile → Export logbook, any time.
  • Delete everything: Profile → Delete my profile.
  • Ask what’s stored about you, or for something to be corrected: Send feedback. If you’re not happy with the answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Keeping it safe

Everything travels over HTTPS. Passwords and sign-in codes are stored only as one-way scrambles, and the sign-in cookie can’t be read by scripts on the page. No system is perfect, so keep your certified paper log, and export a copy now and then.

Terms of use

  • Hours to Go is free to use.
  • It is a study and planning aid, not an official record. Your certified personal log and Transport Canada decide what counts. Check anything that matters with your flight training unit.
  • The requirements come from Transport Canada’s CAR Standard 421, as read by the developer. Where the text needed interpreting, the app says so, and mistakes are possible. Don’t rely on it alone for a decision about your training, an exam, a flight test or a flight.
  • It is provided as it is, without any warranty. As far as the law allows, the developer isn’t responsible for any loss from using it, including lost data.
  • Use it only for your own logbook. Don’t try to get into other people’s profiles, overload the service, or copy it in bulk. A profile used that way may be removed.
  • Hours to Go may change, or stop. If it ever stops, everyone with a profile will be emailed in good time to export their logbook.
  • This page may change. The date at the top says when it last did.
